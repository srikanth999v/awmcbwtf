Our Services

End-to-end biomedical waste management solutions compliant with BMW Rules, 2016 and CPCB guidelines.

Collection & Transport

GPS-monitored dedicated vehicles collect segregated biomedical waste from 868+ HCFs daily across 46 mandals.

Incineration

State-of-the-art incinerators operating at 850-1100C for yellow category waste with continuous emission monitoring.

Autoclaving & Shredding

Pre-vacuum autoclaves with chemical indicator testing for red & white category waste sterilization.

Barcode Tracking

100% barcode-based tracking from generation to disposal, integrated with CPCB systems.

Recycling & Disinfection

Chemical disinfection, plastic recycling after treatment, and glass/metal recovery for sound disposal.

Compliance Reporting

Auto-generated daily, monthly & annual reports (Form IV) as per BMW Rules, 2016.

Training & Awareness

Regular training programs for HCF staff on segregation, handling, and safety as per BMW Rules.

24x7 Grievance Support

Round-the-clock emergency response and grievance redressal for all connected HCFs.

Effluent Treatment

On-site ETP ensuring treated effluent meets prescribed discharge standards before release.
